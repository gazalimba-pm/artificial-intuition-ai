      <div class="body-text">
        <p>Artificial Intelligence has come a long way in recognizing patterns, generating content and reasoning. But people don't decide on logic alone.</p>
        <p>Intuitive signals precede conscious thought that often guide our biggest breakthroughs in entrepreneurship, scientific discovery, and crisis management. Intuition stems from our ability to recognize patterns subconsciously, weigh emotions, and infer from lived experiences.</p>
        <p>Today's Artificial Intelligence systems is yet to catch up with this fundamental dimension of human cognition: intuitive decision-making. This is not an engineering gap. It's a scientific one.</p>
      </div>
    </section>

      <h2>The Beginning</h2>
      <div class="body-text">
        <p>Artificial Intuition Research Pvt. Ltd. is grounded on a specific falsifiable hypothesis: Intuition is scientifically definable and eventually engineerable. Human intuition is not mystical, and the right scientific language to model it has been missing. This is the founding insight.</p>
        <p>The aim is not to mimic consciousness or recreate human identity but to explore how Artificial Intelligence in the future, might go beyond explicit reasoning to embrace sub-conscious intuitive way of making decisions.</p>
